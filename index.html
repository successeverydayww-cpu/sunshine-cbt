Try a sample question

A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 20 m/s in 5 seconds. What is its acceleration?

Correct: B. 4 m/s²
Acceleration = change in velocity ÷ time = 20 ÷ 5 = 4 m/s². Every question in the app carries a step-by-step breakdown like this — and you can ask the Prime Scholars Agent to go deeper.

Try a sample question

Evaluate 25½ × 82/3.

Correct: C. 20
25½ = 5 and 82/3 = (2³)2/3 = 2² = 4, so 5 × 4 = 20. In the app you can tap the calculator and ask the agent to show the working again.

Try a sample question

Which gas is released when dilute hydrochloric acid reacts with zinc?

Correct: A. Hydrogen
Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂. Zinc displaces hydrogen from the acid, so hydrogen gas bubbles off. Tap the agent in the app for the full theory.
