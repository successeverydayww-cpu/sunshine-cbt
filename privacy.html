Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

1. Who we are

Prime Scholars is a computer-based test (CBT) practice application for students of students of partner schools and anyone preparing for JAMB, WAEC and NECO examinations. It is operated by Samuel Amos, Prime Scholars Learning Technologies.

Email: successeverydayww@gmail.com

2. What information we collect

Account detailsFull name, email address, class (SS1–SS3) and class arm. Your email is used as your unique login identity.
Profile detailsOptional: age range, gender, phone number, guardian phone number, target exam, chosen subjects, short bio and a profile photo.
PasswordYour password is stored only as a salted SHA-256 hash. We never store or see your plain password.
Verification codesA 6-digit code is emailed to you when you sign up or reset your password. Codes expire after 10 minutes.
Use activityWe count your logins and daily practice streaks so the school and you can see engagement and progress.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your student account and sign you in.
  • To send verification and password-reset codes to your email.
  • To show your progress, streaks and results inside the app.
  • To show school administrators anonymous, aggregate numbers of students, subscriptions and engagement for reporting.
  • To contact you or your guardian about your account if needed.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. We do not run advertising or third-party trackers inside the app.

4. Students who are minors

Many of our students are under 18. If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must approve your use of Prime Scholars, including providing their phone number as the guardian contact during sign-up. Guardians may ask us at any time to see, correct or delete their child's information using the contact details above.

5. Payments and subscriptions

If a paid subscription is introduced, payments are processed by a licensed payment provider. We never see or store your card details. We keep a record of who has an active subscription (yes or no) so the app can unlock paid features, and the school and builder receive a documented percentage share of the subscription income.

6. Where your data lives and how it is protected

Account data is stored securely on the application's backend. Access is protected by your password and by rate limits against repeated wrong attempts. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days. Only the school's administrators can see your streak and subscription status; other students cannot see your data.

7. Deleting your information

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open My profile → Danger zone → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, photo and progress. You can also email us at successeverydayww@gmail.com and we will delete your data for you.

8. Third-party services

We use an email delivery service (Brevo) to send verification codes and password resets. Your email address is used only to deliver those messages. We do not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking services.

9. Terms of Service

  • Use the app honestly: one account per student, your real name and email.
  • Do not share your password or attempt to access another student's account.
  • Do not copy, resell or redistribute the questions and lessons outside the app without written permission.
  • Practice questions are original practice material prepared for this app. They are not official examination papers. The app is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with JAMB, WAEC or NECO.
  • Abuse, hacking attempts or harassment lead to account suspension. The owner and school administrators decide on suspension and may reverse it.
  • The service is provided as-is for study practice. We work hard to keep questions accurate, but we are not liable for examination outcomes.

If we make changes we will update this page and the date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
